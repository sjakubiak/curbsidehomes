Alerts are short and need no tap. At most one every 2 minutes; the rest go to your drive log.

For buyers

The price shows up at the curb

Buyers already drive neighbourhoods looking for signs. Curbside tells them the asking price the moment they pass, and one tap later they are on your listing.

For your brokerage

Your name on every alert

Listings appear with your brokerage's name and logo and link straight to the REALTOR.ca listing, as CREA's DDF rules require. Every tap is a warm buyer lead.

Built for the road

Safe by design

Alerts can be read at a glance. One per home, never more than one every two minutes, and a Save for later mode that waits until the driver parks.

All homes, prices and street names in this demo are made up. The live app uses listings from a participating brokerage's DDF feed.
